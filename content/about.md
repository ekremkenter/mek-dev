## The arc (hero paragraph)

I lead the Digital Lab at Turkish Technology — the research scout boat ahead
of a very big ship. Our job is to explore emerging technologies and turn the
promising ones into things Turkish Airlines actually ships.

The clearest example: we built one of the first enterprise MCP platforms in
the world, connecting an airline's core services — flight search, booking,
baggage, city guides — directly to AI assistants, with interactive branded
cards rendered inside the chat. It made Turkish Airlines one of the first
airlines live on both ChatGPT and Claude, and the first airline in
Anthropic's connector directory. One backend, one tool spec, every major AI
agent.

## The shift I write and speak about

Booking engines are moving from forms to conversations. Full-page web is
giving way to chat-native UI. Distribution is becoming a question of which
capabilities you expose, not which channel you own. Recent talks: keynote at
Future of Aviation 2026 in Helsinki ("Chat Is the New Booking Engine") and
"Building Chat-Native UI with MCP Apps" at AWS's Twin Strikers event in
Istanbul.

## The long version

Before this role: 17 years across the stack. CTO at Popupsmart (shipped
LiveChatAI in under two months), lead engineer at BlueReceipt, two
bootstrapped startups of my own, and earlier years at Turkish Airlines
building Boti — the airline's chatbot, live on WhatsApp and Messenger years
before LLMs made conversation easy — and leading the mobile app's cloud
migration to AWS. Earlier still: ad engines serving 150M requests/day and
anti-money-laundering systems for Turkey's Financial Investigation Unit.

That mix — founder instincts inside a 100M-passenger airline — is what the
Digital Lab runs on.
