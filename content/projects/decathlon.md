Three engagements for Decathlon, all on serverless AWS:

1. **Spending Survey App (2018)** — in-store app used in Turkey, Tunisia, and
   Morocco. Android client, serverless Node.js backend, Aurora MySQL.
2. **Customs Pipeline (2019)** — serverless data pipeline (Lambda, S3, SQS)
   with a Flutter Web dashboard for statistics and analysis.
3. **HR Platform (2020)** — React/TypeScript staff panel + Gatsby candidate
   site + serverless backend. (The old /decathlon-hr redirect pointed to
   kesfet.decathlon.com.tr.)

Together these make a strong "trusted repeatedly by an enterprise client"
story.
