Three engagements for Decathlon, all on serverless AWS:

1. **Spending Survey App (2018)** — an in-store app used across Turkey,
   Tunisia, and Morocco. Android client, serverless Node.js backend, Aurora
   MySQL.
2. **Customs Pipeline (2019)** — a serverless data pipeline (Lambda, S3,
   SQS) with a Flutter Web dashboard for statistics and analysis.
3. **HR Platform (2020)** — a React/TypeScript staff panel, a Gatsby
   candidate site, and a serverless backend.

An enterprise client that came back three times — the strongest kind of
reference.
